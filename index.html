What you are seeing

P (blue) is a fixed Gaussian target. Q (green) starts far from P and is linearly interpolated toward it as the slider t moves from 0 to 1 (LERP).

The left chart shows both PDFs updating during the interpolation. The right chart plots Entropy H(P) as a static baseline alongside KL Divergence and Cross-Entropy as continuous functions of t, with a moving marker on each curve plus a vertical tracker line showing the current t.

LERP $\mu_Q=(1-t)\mu_{Q_0}+t\,\mu_P,\quad \sigma_Q=(1-t)\sigma_{Q_0}+t\,\sigma_P$

Entropy $H(P)=\tfrac12\ln\!\big(2\pi e\,\sigma_P^2\big)$

KL Divergence $D_{KL}(P\|Q)=\ln\dfrac{\sigma_Q}{\sigma_P}+\dfrac{\sigma_P^2+(\mu_P-\mu_Q)^2}{2\sigma_Q^2}-\tfrac12$

Cross-Entropy $H(P,Q)=H(P)+D_{KL}(P\|Q)$
